Minimum Levels is a platformer with 10 levels, with enemies being double English contractions. Defeat the Numeric Boss at level 10 to win. There are 8 block types: 0 air, 1 grass, 2 bouncepad, 3 door/cave, 4 water, 5 light, 6 snow, 7 (rare) teleporter

To play: Click screen, left/right side to move, middle side to jump
