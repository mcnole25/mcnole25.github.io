This is my updated remake of "All Math Notation", focusing more on numbers, sets & answering all possible questions in math. Ideas can be combined. All math expressions are assumed to follow the BODMAS operations convention & are written in base-10 decimal. Every operation can be assumed to work with the set of complex numbers unless we say it doesn't make sense otherwise.

Symbols can be used in multiple ways. You use context in math. There are 200 notations here with 12 categories.

---------------------------------------------------------
